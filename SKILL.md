# Text-First Exegesis

Use this directional, non-compensatory hierarchy:

**textual facts > literary facts > historical facts > exegetical viewpoints > theology**

Later layers may refine an earlier layer left open; they may not overturn it by accumulation. If later evidence exposes a missed earlier-layer variable, rerun that earlier layer with evidence proper to it.

## Required products

For every contextual meaning claim, output in this order:

1. **Object declaration**
2. **A - Independent reconstruction**
3. **B - Current frontier**, with separate **B1** and **B2**
4. **Comparison and critique**
5. Theology/canon/reception/pastoral material only when requested

Pure lookup of spelling, parsing, morphology, sigla, or a critical-edition reading may omit the dual track when no meaning claim is requested.

Freeze A before consulting modern secondary scholarship.

## Literary object

**Old Testament**
- Treat each `book x textual tradition` as a separate synchronic literary object.
- Study MT and LXX/Old Greek in parallel when both survive.
- MT: BHS base; prefer BHQ where available.
- LXX/Old Greek: Goettingen where available; otherwise Rahlfs-Hanhart.
- Add DSS or another ancient tradition when materially relevant and recoverable.

**New Testament**
- Use the target book's NA/ECM initial text; prefer ECM where available.
- Treat material judged secondary/post-compositional as textual/transmission history, not part of the Layer 2 literary object.

For Layer 2, the whole locked book is the synchronic literary object; the target passage is a window into it. Read `references/literary-analysis.md` before Layer 2.

## Track A - AI diagnostic reconstruction

A is deliberately low-trust and reference-only. It may use primary texts, critical editions, manuscripts, primary-language corpora, and first-order historical evidence. It may not use named scholars, remembered consensus, commentary conclusions, or modern secondary-source search results.

Run:

1. **Text** - establish wording and relevant variants.
2. **Literature** - apply `references/literary-analysis.md`.
3. **Object-internal testing** - test decisive terms, relations, participants, and literary patterns within the same locked book/tradition.
4. **Rivals** - when a real dispute exists, construct at least two serious object-internal models and identify the first discriminating evidence.
5. **History** - add wider language, culture, archaeology, intertexts, other books/traditions, composition/redaction, and transmission. Intertexts are historical evidence; they do not decide the target book's literary relation by themselves.
6. **Freeze A** - state conclusion, alternatives, unresolved questions, and a compact `TARGET INFERENCE CHAIN`.

## Track B - current-frontier reconstruction

Begin only after A is frozen. Read `references/frontier-search.md`.

Track B contains two independent judgments:

### B1 - Independent five-layer evidential audit

Audit every major recovered proposal through:

**Textual -> Literary -> Historical -> Exegetical -> Theological/Canonical**

Author identity, Hermeneia status, venue, prestige, recency, uptake, and provenance contribute zero weight to B1. Preserve the audit result even when B2 ranks that proposal differently.

### B2 - Scholarly authority competition

Rank recovered scholarly positions using the project rules in `references/frontier-search.md`.

Core policy:
- authority attaches to the **target book's verified Hermeneia assignee as a scholar**, not only to the Hermeneia volume;
- recover the assignee's relevant formal monographs, articles, chapters, commentaries, reference works, and theses/dissertations, including older work when needed;
- when the assignee's position develops, use the **newest recoverable target-relevant formal statement** as the current author position and treat older positions as development history;
- a recovered Hermeneia-author position is presumptively controlling in B2;
- only an eligible **elite rebuttal source** may displace it: default elite tier = **Hermeneia, AYB, JBL, WUNT, FAT**, plus only clearly comparable top specialist journals or major research reference works;
- elite venue alone is insufficient: the source must make an **explicit, reasoned challenge** to the target-relevant Hermeneia claim or decisive inference and argue for rejection, replacement, or material restriction;
- ordinary scholarship may disagree, support B1, map influence, or supply objections, but cannot overturn Hermeneia authority in B2;
- under `CENSUS INCOMPLETE`, B2 may challenge Hermeneia only if such an elite explicit challenge has already been recovered; otherwise Hermeneia remains controlling.

B1 and B2 may disagree. When they do, label the result **`AUTHORITY / EVIDENCE-AUDIT DIVERGENCE`** and report both conclusions without harmonizing them.

## Track B source policy

- **Formal scholarship** includes academic monographs, peer-reviewed journal articles, academic edited-volume chapters, technical commentaries, critical editions/apparatuses, formally published reference works, and formally deposited research theses/dissertations. Web delivery does not change formal status.
- Blogs, general webpages, ministry sites, newsletters, social media, podcasts, videos, lectures, sermons, conference talks, forums, and similar informal material cannot carry substantive B interpretation. Official institutional/publisher/author webpages may be used for metadata and discovery.
- Verify an earned research doctorate (PhD, DPhil, ThD, or genuine equivalent) before an author may carry B2's recommendation.
- Apply the explicit series, author, and platform blacklists and their usage rules from `references/frontier-search.md`. That file is the single source of truth for blacklist membership.
- Apply a strong negative B2 provenance prior to conservative institutional/publication provenance and to recurrent confessional-theological backflow into historical-literary exegesis. Also normally flag explicit attacks on BHQ, ECM, Hermeneia, or AYB as a conservative-provenance signal; exempt Hermeneia assignees from that criticism-based flag. A Hermeneia assignment overrides the generic conservative-provenance penalty, but not the explicit author blacklist or doctorate gate.
- Ordinary B2 contests use a **modernity/revision prior**: among comparably qualified live proposals, prefer newer substantive revisions supported by admissible evidence and strong scholarly provenance. This prior sits below Hermeneia-author authority.

## Discovery principle

**Discovery is authority-neutral.** Hermeneia receives mandatory visibility and priority, but expected B2 rank must not decide who is searched.

Use a short Hermeneia seed sweep, then run authority-blind searches based on the text, passage, unit, whole-book structure, decisive terms, and live rival models. Search user-named scholars and scholars repeatedly identified as substantive rivals, originators, or major citation-network nodes regardless of expected B2 standing. Return to the full Hermeneia author corpus after the blind discovery lane.

The ordinary frontier window is the rolling **15 years**. Older ordinary work may be recovered when recent formal scholarship treats it as a live predecessor, originator, or direct target of debate; it informs proposal genealogy and B1 without automatically gaining current ordinary-tier B2 weight.

## Named-model questions

When the user asks which named interpretive or doctrinal model follows, define the models by their minimal distinguishing commitments and map **A, B1, and B2 separately** onto those commitments. Preserve the scholar's actual thesis; use `<model>-equivalent` when the classification is inferential rather than the scholar's own label.

## Final comparison

Keep A frozen. Compare three questions explicitly:

- What did A independently reconstruct?
- What does B1's evidence audit find?
- What does B2's scholarly authority competition control?

The user-facing scholarly recommendation follows B2, while any B1 disagreement remains visible and fully stated.
