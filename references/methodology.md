# Methodology Reference

Use this file to explain or audit the method. Live exegesis follows `SKILL.md`; Track B additionally loads `frontier-search.md`.

## 1. Core architecture

The project uses a directional five-layer hierarchy:

**Textual > Literary > Historical > Exegetical viewpoints > Theology**

The hierarchy governs evidence. Later layers may refine open questions but may not compensate for a failed earlier layer.

The literary object is operationally fixed before interpretation: OT `book x textual tradition`; NT NA/ECM initial-text book. The whole locked book is the synchronic literary object for Layer 2.

## 2. Why A and B differ

**A** is a frozen, low-trust AI reconstruction from primary evidence before modern scholarship. It is diagnostic, not advisory.

**B** reconstructs the scholarly frontier and produces two separate judgments:

- **B1** asks which proposal best fits the project's five-layer evidence method.
- **B2** asks which scholarly position controls under the project's declared source-authority policy.

B1 and B2 are intentionally independent. Authority cannot repair B1; the model's B1 preference cannot manufacture B2 rebuttal standing.

## 3. Literary discipline

Within Layer 2 use:

**genre > whole-book macrostructure > unit relation > local discourse/form > syntax/lexeme**

Keep discourse order, internal unit chronology, unit opening/endpoint horizons, represented event order, and logical order distinct. Local sequence needs positive whole-book warrant before it controls event chronology. Intertexts belong to the historical layer and cannot substitute for target-book literary evidence.

## 4. Track B research discipline

Discovery is authority-neutral. Hermeneia receives mandatory visibility and B2 priority, while ordinary and opposing scholars must still be discoverable through text/problem searches, user-named researcher sweeps, and citation networks.

The ordinary frontier window is rolling 15 years. Older work re-enters as lineage/background when recent formal scholarship still adopts, revises, or directly contests it. Hermeneia-author corpus recovery may reach farther back to reconstruct the assignee's current position.

Formal scholarship includes monographs, peer-reviewed articles, academic chapters, technical commentaries, critical editions, reference works, and formally deposited research theses/dissertations. Informal web discourse cannot carry substantive B interpretation.

## 5. Hermeneia policy

The authority prior attaches to the current target-book Hermeneia assignee's relevant formal scholarly corpus. When the author's view develops, the newest recoverable target-relevant formal statement represents the current position.

In B2, that position is controlling unless an eligible elite source - Hermeneia, AYB, JBL, WUNT, FAT, or a clearly comparable top specialist venue/reference work - makes an explicit reasoned challenge to the target claim or decisive inference and argues for rejection, replacement, or material restriction.

Ordinary disagreement does not acquire rebuttal standing by quantity, recency, or B1 strength. Under an incomplete census, only an already recovered elite explicit challenge can open a provisional B2 challenge.

## 6. Provenance policy

B2 applies the project's doctorate gate, explicit blacklists, conservative-provenance penalty (including the criticism-based BHQ/ECM/Hermeneia/AYB signal, with the Hermeneia-assignee exemption), confessional-backflow penalty, and ordinary modernity/revision prior. These are declared meta-epistemic preferences, not textual/literary/historical evidence.

## 7. Output logic

Keep A, B1, and B2 separate. For named-model questions, map each product to the model's minimal distinguishing commitments rather than relying only on the scholar's preferred label.

When B1 and B2 disagree, show `AUTHORITY / EVIDENCE-AUDIT DIVERGENCE`. The practical scholarly recommendation follows B2; the B1 evidence judgment remains visible.
