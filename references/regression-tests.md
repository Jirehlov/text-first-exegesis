# Offline Evaluation Protocol

Never load this file during live exegesis. Keep passage-specific answer keys outside the installed skill.

## Track A pass

A held-out run should:
1. use only layers 1-3 before freezing A;
2. treat the whole target book as the literary object;
3. keep disputed dependencies unresolved until independently tested;
4. generate serious rivals without importing scholarly labels as premises;
5. separate literary and historical evidence;
6. state an independent conclusion and uncertainty without giving a substantive disputed A conclusion 'high confidence'.

Fail A if modern scholarship, remembered consensus, or B supplies the decisive candidate or ranking before A is frozen.

## Track B pass

B should:
1. begin only after A is frozen;
2. complete the mandatory discovery sweep in `frontier-search.md`: exact passage, whole literary unit, composition/source/redaction/original-form blind-spot search, decisive control variables, adversarial challenge to the provisional leading B-model, field-hub search/seeding, and the platform matrix; meet the discovery-ledger recall floor before provenance/method pruning;
3. enforce a rolling 15-year eligibility gate for modern secondary scholarship (current year minus 15, inclusive), while exempting primary/first-order evidence;
4. audit author provenance for major directly relevant candidates before calling an author established-frontier; require a sustained record of several field-relevant research books/monographs with leading university presses (Cambridge, Oxford, Yale, Harvard, Edinburgh, or comparable), while allowing an emerging proposal to enter through demonstrated current uptake without upgrading the author;
5. use Hermeneia/EKK/IECOT as strong discovery priors, AYB as a positive prior, ICC as neutral/variable, and all other commentary series without automatic frontier status; use at least two recent field-level handbooks/companions/state-of-research volumes as researcher hubs when two exist, treat every editor as a mandatory researcher seed, search relevant contributors, and complete separate Cambridge and Oxford platform passes plus two additional high-level platform passes before provenance qualification; then trace directly relevant recent citation chains until serious live models stop emerging;
6. recover each major directly relevant proposal's **thesis, target unit, primary evidence, and decisive inference** from frontier-eligible material before counting it as mapped;
7. record **recency, author provenance, current uptake, direct relevance, and method compatibility**, then derive frontier status from those fields;
8. enforce direct relevance as a gate: prestigious adjacent-topic sources may supply background but cannot carry the target conclusion;
9. distinguish frontier from consensus and verify distribution before claiming consensus;
10. treat method compatibility as an admissibility/audit gate rather than a score for ranking otherwise compatible proposals;
11. steelman every major method-compatible proposal that may affect the conclusion by recovering its strongest target-book case, decisive variable/construction, handling of obvious counterevidence, and frontier-eligible direct criticism/uptake;
12. enforce the no-unrecovered-objection rule and the rebuttal burden: do not let the model's own alternative reading defeat a steelmanned method-compatible frontier proposal without a direct frontier-eligible same-layer rebuttal or a clear independently checkable first-three-layer error that recent defenders fail to answer;
13. decompose each decisive proposal into textual, literary, historical, exegetical, and theological/canonical components, then re-adjudicate in that order;
14. enforce non-reversal: historical/intertextual evidence may refine surviving literary models or trigger a literary rerun, but may not directly overturn a literary freeze or transfer an external source's referent/function into the target work;
15. report influential method-incompatible views fairly, identify the backflow/category error, and retest their remaining primary-evidence case under layers 1-3;
16. when the rebuttal burden is unmet, state that the compatible frontier remains unresolved rather than forcing a winner;
17. freeze B without using A as evidence.

Fail B if it:
- counts pre-cutoff secondary scholarship as current frontier evidence, consensus evidence, or a carrier of the target conclusion;
- reconstructs a current proposal from pre-cutoff scholarship when no frontier-eligible recent carrier has been recovered;
- labels an author established-frontier without auditing a sustained, field-relevant publication record, or confuses one prestigious publication with sustained provenance;
- treats Hermeneia/EKK/IECOT/AYB/ICC or any other commentary series as automatic proof of frontier status, especially treating ICC as uniformly frontier;
- excludes a genuinely live emerging proposal solely because its author lacks established-frontier provenance even when strong frontier-eligible current uptake is present;
- treats venue, series, author prestige, provenance, current uptake, or recency as exegetical proof rather than discovery/coverage metadata;
- misses a serious live alternative because it searched only the target lemma/title wording or stopped before the adversarial route;
- applies author provenance or series/venue reputation before candidate discovery and thereby narrows the search space;
- omits the composition blind-spot route, fails the discovery-ledger recall floor without recording a real limitation, fails to search every available hub editor as a seed, or omits the separate Cambridge/Oxford platform passes when relevant;
- treats failure of a lemma-level query as evidence that no serious proposal exists without escalating to passage, unit, composition, and researcher-network searches;
- uses an adjacent-topic source as though it directly resolves the target dispute;
- claims the field is concentrated or calls a view consensus before completing the coverage gate;
- omits method audit for a decisive scholarly proposal;
- discovers a major directly relevant scholar/work but never recovers what that proposal actually argues;
- treats a review, abstract, publisher description, or bibliographic mention as a substitute for the proposal itself;
- treats method compatibility as a scalar score that itself ranks two otherwise compatible proposals;
- rejects or downgrades a method-compatible frontier proposal on the basis of A, model intuition, a few apparently contrary passages, or a more familiar reading without meeting the rebuttal burden;
- calls an apparent counterexample decisive without recovering or seriously searching for the proposal's own response to it;
- claims to have evaluated a major method-compatible proposal without recovering its strongest target-book evidence and inferential hinge;
- forces a substantive B winner when no frontier-eligible same-layer rebuttal or clear independently checkable first-three-layer error has been established;
- treats recognition of an intertext as automatic transfer of the source text's referent, chronology, ontology, or function;
- lets historical/intertextual evidence directly overturn a literary freeze instead of reopening and rerunning the literary layer;
- aggregates lower-layer evidence against higher-layer evidence as though the hierarchy were a flat vote.

## Separation pass

After both freezes, comparison must identify convergence or divergence and let B adversarially criticize A for missed live models, omitted/misweighted evidence, loaded assumptions, and overconfidence. Neither track may be rewritten to agree with the other. A missed frontier candidate in A is diagnostic, not a reason to alter A. Criticism must respect the same-layer rule: A literary ranking may be corrected only by a stronger target-book literary case, not by historical/intertextual evidence alone; later evidence may trigger a rerun but cannot substitute for it. When A conflicts with a steelmanned method-compatible frontier proposal that has not been adequately rebutted, the comparison must lower A's confidence or mark A under-supported; it may not use A's independent reading as the rebuttal.

## Global fail

Fail a run if theology/canon controls historical exegesis, cross-book material is treated as literary evidence of the target book, a later evidence layer is presented as though it were an earlier fact, or lower-layer evidence is allowed to reverse a higher-layer freeze without an explicit same-layer rerun.
